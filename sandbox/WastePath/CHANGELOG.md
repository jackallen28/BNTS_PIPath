# 0.3.0 — restore the original ML Camera workflow

- Restore original full-frame largest-object segmentation, colour/shape scaling,
  weighted neighbour voting, smoothing, photo quiz and novelty threshold.
- Remove the people training category, required item groups and extra geometric /
  motion rejection gates. Require only ten photos of Waste and ten of Recycling.
- Average ten background frames and add the original threshold adjustment.
- Move directly to Live after training. Open the recycling servo on a consistent
  recognised result; flash the LED for waste or unrecognised/uncertain items.
- Stop the LED on an empty table or camera loss. Preserve custom GPIO settings,
  explicit hardware enabling, cleanup and the independent watchdog.
- Back up old-format cropped training and request one new full-frame training set.
- Add regression results generated by running the user's uploaded original program.
